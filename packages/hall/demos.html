Demos

Every scene, one link away

The showcase plays one film. These open the hall on a chosen scene: a story, a crowd, a repo, an hour. Each one comes with the Director's controls in Settings, so you can change the story, the hour, the weather and how the Bard films it.

Stories

Simulated guilds, each a told story with its own cast.

  • OpenCode · five acts · about 17 minutes

    The Saga

    The showcase's own film, with every lever in reach. One feature, “make Quill work offline”, from the quest to the clean sweep, told by a guild of OpenCode agents.

    What it shows

    • A plan asked for and approved: pleas wait for you
    • Red tests bring a storm and a dragon; the fallen rise as skeletons
    • Every world event fires on its own: nothing is forced
    • IDawn
    • IIThe forge
    • IIIThe storm
    • IVThe mending
    • VNightfall
    Open The Saga ?story=saga

  • One conversation

    A party

    One guildmaster and the adventurers it sends out. Each quest is taken from the board, walked to its station and worked in front of you.

    What it shows

    • Every tool call is a deed you can see
    • Click anyone to follow them and read their dossier
    Open A party ?story=party

  • Two harnesses at once

    Claude Code and OpenCode

    An OpenCode party and a Claude Code party share one island, each under its own banner, each with its own guildmaster.

    What it shows

    • Two sources in one world
    • Parties keep their colours and their own stations
    Open Claude Code and OpenCode ?story=factions

  • A repo's sea

    GitHub seas

    A party with its GitHub sea beside it, from the first push to the release: the repo's life as ships coming and going.

    What it shows

    • Pull requests anchor offshore; a release arrives as a galleon
    • The lighthouse pulses red when CI fails
    Open GitHub seas ?story=seas

Repos

Real repositories, read from their history and grown into islands.

  • The Harbour, at sea

    An archipelago of real repos

    The chronicled repos from the Harbour as islands around the guild's own. Sail from one to the next.

    What it shows

    • Each island grown from a repo's real history
    • M for the map, 1–6 to sail to an island, 0 for home
    Open An archipelago of real repos ?archipelago

  • A growth timelapse

    React grows from its first commit

    React's whole history as a timelapse: every folder a district, every release a milestone, every contributor a townsfolk.

    What it shows

    • Years of commits in a couple of minutes
    • Milestones and captions along the tape
    Open React grows from its first commit ?repo=facebook/react&grow

  • Any public repo

    A repo as an island

    A public GitHub repo grown into a voxel island: its folders become districts. The same tree always grows the same island. This one is mcpx.

    What it shows

    • Read from the public file tree, never the code
    • Grow your own from “Your repo as an island”
    Open A repo as an island ?repo=Codestz/mcpx

The world

How the island is drawn, how many it holds, and the sky over it.

  • Stress test

    A crowd of 300

    Three hundred adventurers on one island, every one animated. Baked bone textures, mesh LOD and instancing keep it smooth.

    What it shows

    • 300 skinned characters in a handful of draw calls
    • Turn on Stats for nerds to watch the frame rate
    Open A crowd of 300 ?story=rush&n=300

  • Two renderers, one island

    WebGPU and WebGL

    The same hall on three.js's WebGPU renderer, or on WebGL, the default. Same scene, same shaders, written once in TSL.

    What it shows

    • One codebase, two backends
    • How they compare is on How it's built

    Needs WebGPU (recent Chrome, Edge or Safari). Elsewhere the hall falls back to WebGL. A preview: ambient occlusion isn't drawn there yet. Open each in its own tab with Settings › Stats for nerds on, and compare.

    Open in WebGPU WebGPU and WebGL ?renderer=webgpu

  • Time and sky

    Night and weather

    The island at ten at night, in a storm: lamps along the paths, rain, lightning over the sea. Change the hour and the weather from the Director's controls.

    What it shows

    • A full day and night cycle, and four weathers
    • In Auto, failures bring the clouds
    Open Night and weather ?hour=22&weather=storm
